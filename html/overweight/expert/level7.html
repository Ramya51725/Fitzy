Skipping without rope

Instructions

  • Place your arms at your sides and pretend to hold a skipping
    rope handle in each hand.

  • Jump and alternately land on the balls of your feet, rotating your
    wrists at the same time as if you were spinning a rope.

Breathing Tip

  • Breathe in through your nose and out through your mouth
    at a regular pace. This helps you avoid becoming too winded.
