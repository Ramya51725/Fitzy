Instructions

  • Place one arm across your body, parallel to the ground,
    then use the other arm to pull the parallel arm
    toward your chest.

  • Hold for a while, switch arms and repeat the exercise. Keep
    the inside arm straight during the exercise.

Breathing Tip

  • Exhale slowly as you pull your arms and stretch your shoulder.

  • Inhale deeply as you finish stretch.
