Instructions

  • Stand with your feet shoulder width apart and your arms
    stretched forward, then lower your body until your thighs
    are parallel with the floor.

  • Your knees should be extended in the same direction as
    your toes. Return to the start position and do the next rep.

  • This works the thighs, hips buttocks, quads,
    hamstrings and lower body.
