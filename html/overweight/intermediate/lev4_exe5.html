Dumbell punch

Instructions

  • Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your knees
    slightly bent. Hold a dumbbell in each hand and
    clench your fists in front of your face.

  • Extend one arm forward in a punching motion while
    turning your palm down. Take the arm back and repeat
    with the other arm.

Breathing Tips

  • Inhale deeply through the nose as you prepare to punch.
