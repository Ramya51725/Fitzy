Side scissor

Instructions

  • Lie on your back with arms at sides, palms
    down and legs extended.

  • Lift your legs and cross the right thigh over the
    left, then reverse the exercise.

  • Make sure your back is always flat.

  • It's a great exercise for the obliques and rectus abdominis.
