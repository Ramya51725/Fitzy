Russian twist

Instructions

  • Sit on the floor with your knees bent, feet lifted a little
    bit and back tilted backwards.

  • Then hold your hands together and twist from side to side.

Breathing Tip

  • When you're twisting your upper body, exhale and
    engage your core muscles. This can help you twist
    more effectively and prevent injury.
