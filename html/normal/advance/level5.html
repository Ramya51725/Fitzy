Instructions

  • Lean forward, bend your knees and your elbows.

  • Extend your arms behind you and squeeze your triceps.
    Please make your arms parallel to the ground,
    when extending them.

Breathing Tip

  • Take a deep breath in before starting and exhale
    as you extend your arms.

  • Inhale as you bring your arms back to the starting position
