Instructions

  • Lie on the floor with your hands behind your ears.
    Raise your knees and close your right elbow toward your left
    knee, then close your left elbow toward your right knee.
    Repeat the exercise.

Breathing Tip

  • When you're lifting your upper body, exhale and
    engage your core muscles. This can help you lift more
    effectively and prevent injury.
