Instructions

  • Start in the regular push-up position,
    but with one hand in front of the other.

  • Then do a push-up and switch the other hand in front.

  • Remember to keep your body straight.

Breathing Tip

  • Inhale through the nose as you lower your body.

  • exhale through the mouth as you push back up
