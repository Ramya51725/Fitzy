Arm raises

Instructions

  • Stand on the floor with your arms extended straight
    forward at shoulder height.

  • Exhale, raise your arms above your head. Inhale, return
    to the start position and repeat.

Breathing Tip

  • Exhale deeply through the mouth as you raise your arms.

  • Inhale forcefully through the nose as you lower your arms.
