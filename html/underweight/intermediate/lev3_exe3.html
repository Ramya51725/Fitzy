V up exercise

Instructions

  • Lie on your back with your arms and legs
    extended and your legs squeezed together.

  • Raise your upper body and legs, use your arms
    to touch your toes, then go back to the start
    position and repeat the exercise.

Breathing Tip

  • Exhale as you raise your upper body and legs.

  • Inhale as you put your upper body and legs down.
