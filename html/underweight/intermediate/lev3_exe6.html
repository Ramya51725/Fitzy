Crossover crunch

Instructions

  • Raise and twist your torso so your right elbow moves to
    meet your left knee. Repeat with the other side.

  • Lie on your back with your knees bent and your
    hands behind your ears.

Breathing Tip

  • Inhale deeply as you lie on your back with your
    knees bent and feet flat on the ground.

  • Exhale as you crunch up and bring your right
    elbow to your left knee
