Instructions

  • Start with your hands and feet touching the floor,
    body bent and butt up in an upside down 'V' shape.

  • Then bend your elbows to bring your body towards the floor.

  • When your body is close to the floor, raise your upper
    body up as far as possible. Then return to the original
    position and repeat.
