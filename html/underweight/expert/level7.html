Floor slides

Instructions

  • Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat on the floor.

  • Put your arms straight over your head.

  • Slide your forearms down at 90 degree angles,
    then slide them up. Repeat the exercise.

Breathing Tip

  • Take a deep breath in as you slide your arms up
    towards your head.
