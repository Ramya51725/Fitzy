Abdominal crunches

Instructions

  • Lie on your back with your knees bent and your
    arms stretched forward.

  • Then lift your upper body off the floor. Hold for a few
    seconds and slowly return.

  • It primarily works the rectus abdominis muscle
    and the obliques.

Breathing tip

  • Inhaling as you lower back down helps you maintain control
    and avoid straining your back.
